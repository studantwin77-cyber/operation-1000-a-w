Minimums and conduct

✓
Weekly minimums

At least 2 study sessions each week, 30 minutes each, and 3 physical activity sessions each week, at least 20 minutes each. These are requirements, not bonus points.

Ⅱ
Breaks and emergencies

Take a break when mentally exhausted. In an emergency, pause the challenge and freeze all scoring until both players agree it is okay to resume.

▦
Days off

Each player gets 10 planned days off across the school year. Record the date ahead of time; once entered, it cannot be changed. Emergencies do not count toward the 10 days.
